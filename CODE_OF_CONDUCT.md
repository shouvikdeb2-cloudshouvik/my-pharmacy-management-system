# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free place for everyone, whatever their background or experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent; ask when something is unclear.
- Stay on topic in issues and pull requests.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination based on race, gender, sexuality, disability, age, religion, nationality, or any other characteristic.
- Doxxing, threats, or sharing private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, spam, or trolling.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block users who break these rules. Report problems privately to the maintainer ([@shouvikdeb2-cloudshouvik](https://github.com/shouvikdeb2-cloudshouvik)) through GitHub. Reports are handled in confidence.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, and discussions.
